Step 1 · PDFsam trim (3 min)

  1. Open PDFsam Basic (pdfsam.org).
  2. Organize → open group eDoc.
  3. Delete pages 1-2 and 66-end.
  4. Save as Guests_Only.pdf.

Step 2 · Split every 3 pages (2 min)

  1. PDFsam → Split.
  2. Upload Guests_Only.pdf.
  3. Choose every 3 pages.
  4. You get 21 files in order.

Step 3 · Excel + Gmail (ongoing)

  1. Open Raj_eDoc_Guest_Tracker.csv in Excel.
  2. Rename each PDF to the filename in column D.
  3. Email one guest PDF per person. Mark Sent = Y.

Before every send

  1. PDF name matches recipient.
  2. Never send the full 93-page eDoc.
  3. Optional: attach 00_Group_Travel_Reference.pdf (pp 66-93).
